## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an authoritative, institutional, and modern industrial standard engineered for enterprise logistics, bulk freight, and supply chain governance across Northern Nigeria's primary trade corridors.

The visual tone communicates absolute operational certainty, heavy asset security, and administrative rigor. Designed for procurement heads, industrial manufacturers, fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG) distributors, and compliance regulators, the interface strips away superfluous embellishment in favor of structural clarity, precise spatial alignment, and data legibility.

### Design Movements & Philosophy
- **Corporate & Modern Industrial:** Anchored by enterprise structural discipline, combining deep institutional navy tones with industrial safety accents reminiscent of freight transport markings, container seals, and digital telematics consoles.
- **Architectural Utility:** Emphasizes high-density data tables, verifiable tracking timelines, strict status indicators, and audit-ready reporting surfaces.
- **Institutional Weight:** Delivers confidence through disciplined borders, crisp micro-elevations, and tabular typographic alignment capable of handling complex freight manifests, billing sheets, and multi-hub transit statuses without visual fatigue.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sense of institutional reliability and operational safety.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0B192C` - Deep Navy):** Represents stability, governance, and institutional authority. Used for commanding structural headers, primary enterprise CTAs, top navigation frames, and high-impact text.
- **Secondary (`#FF7A00` - Industrial Safety Amber/Orange):** Evokes active transit, machinery, telematics alerts, and mission-critical actions. Used purposefully for prime interaction states, critical route milestones, dispatch actions, and shipment alerts. Never used for decorative washes; its presence signals movement or immediate action.
- **Tertiary (`#1E3E62` - Industrial Steel Blue):** Serves as an analytical bridge between dark primary surfaces and data visualization, selected row states, and secondary telemetry data.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` - Slate Core):** Grounded by clean light backdrops (`#F8FAFC`, `#FFFFFF`) and defined by crisp architectural borders (`#E2E8F0`). Secondary slate tones provide structured data labels, metadata strings, and table dividers.

### Semantic Tones
- **Success (`#047857` / `#ECFDF5`):** Cargo cleared, delivered on time, inspection approved.
- **Warning (`#B45309` / `#FFFBEB`):** Transit bottleneck, weighbridge delay, customs review.
- **Critical / Danger (`#B91C1C` / `#FEF2F2`):** Fleet breakdown, route hazard, unauthorized detour.
- **Information (`#0369A1` / `#F0F9FF`):** Manifest logged, scheduled dispatch, checkpoint logged.

## Typography

The type system prioritizes micro-legibility and vertical rhythm across complex operational dashboards and client manifests. Built completely on `Inter`, the neutral grotesque structure prevents visual distortion when viewing dense numeric data, consignment waybills, and coordinates.

### Guidelines
- **Tabular Figures:** All numeric fields (metric values, tonnage figures, currency values in NGN/USD, GPS coordinates, ETA timestamps) must activate `font-feature-settings: "tnum"` to maintain strict tabular vertical alignment.
- **Hierarchy Enforcement:** Section titles and critical haulage route titles utilize medium-to-bold weights with slight negative letter tracking to retain solid visual containment.
- **Micro-labels & Tags:** Labels below `12px` (`label-sm`) require uppercase rendering with expanded letter spacing (`+0.04em`) to ensure legibility on low-contrast warehouse displays or handheld mobile terminal screens.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by a strict, responsive 12-column grid optimized for horizontal data containment and functional enterprise dashboards.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, `gutter-lg` (2rem), `margin-lg` (3rem). Accommodates dense operational split-views (e.g., interactive route maps alongside haulage manifests and load-bay capacity lists).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, `gutter` (1.5rem), `margin` (2rem). Metric cards reflow to a 2x2 grid; freight management tables enable horizontal scroll with frozen ID columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `gutter-sm` (1rem), `margin-sm` (1rem). Quotation forms and operational waybill workflows compress into single-column vertical flows.

### Spacing Principles
- **Predictable 4px Base Unit:** Internal spacing leverages structural intervals: 4px (`space-xs`) for micro-badge containment, 8px (`space-sm`) for input-to-label alignment, 16px (`space-md`) for standard card interiors, 24px (`space-lg`) between related components, and 32px (`space-xl`) between distinct analytical panels.
- **Vertical Density:** Dense table rows and data-heavy summaries default to `0.5rem` (`space-sm`) vertical padding to maximize visible screen real estate without clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on exaggerated skeuomorphism or deep diffused drop shadows. Instead, it utilizes structural containment via crisp slate boundaries paired with subtle tonal layering and razor-sharp micro-shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Main background `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation. Clean architectural base.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Foundation):** White surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) bound by a crisp 1px solid border (`#E2E8F0`). Subtle ambient drop shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.05), 0px 1px 2px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Interactive Cards, Active Filter Bars):** Elevated interactive elements. 1px solid border (`#CBD5E1`), shadow: `0px 4px 6px -1px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.08), 0px 2px 4px -2px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Overlays, Quotation Modals, Waybill Drawers):** Floating system components. 1px border (`#94A3B8`), shadow: `0px 12px 24px -4px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.12), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.06)`.

### Border-First Discipline
Every elevated layer preserves clear structural borders. Boundaries must always be distinct, assuring operators that interactive data blocks are compartmentalized and stable.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a calibrated `Soft` roundedness profile (`roundedness: 1`), conveying enterprise precision, industrial sturdiness, and administrative discipline.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Base UI Elements (`rounded: 0.25rem / 4px`):** Buttons, input fields, table cell badges, dropdown items, and control checkboxes. Gives inputs a crisp, mechanical edge without feeling dated or sharp.
- **Structured Cards & Panels (`rounded-lg: 0.5rem / 8px`):** Metric panels, corridor route overview cards, and quote construction containers.
- **Dialogs & Overlays (`rounded-xl: 0.75rem / 12px`):** Waybill inspection modals, telematics popovers, and large enterprise sheets.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Exclusively reserved for high-visibility operational status badges (e.g., "IN TRANSIT", "WEIGHBRIDGE CLEARED", "DELAYED") to visually distinguish them from actionable buttons and rectangular data inputs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0B192C`, text `#FFFFFF`, border none, border-radius `0.25rem`. Hover: `#1E3E62`. Focus-visible: 2px offset ring in `#FF7A00`.
- **Secondary (Action / Transit Accent):** Background `#FF7A00`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `0.25rem`. Hover: `#E65C00`. Used for "Book Freight", "Confirm Dispatch", and "Request Instant Quote".
- **Outline / Operational:** Background transparent, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, text `#0B192C`. Hover: `#F1F5F9`.

### Input Fields & Quotation Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, border-radius `0.25rem`, vertical padding `0.625rem`, horizontal padding `0.875rem`.
- Focus state: Border shifts to `#0B192C` with a 1px outline of `#0B192C`.
- Route picker inputs (e.g., "Origin: Idu Depot, Abuja", "Destination: Bompai Industrial Zone, Kano") integrate trailing status icons indicating corridor clearance.

### Metric Cards (KPI Displays)
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, border-radius `0.5rem`, padding `1.25rem`.
- Structure: Micro-label header (`label-sm`, `#64748B`), bold numeric display (`headline-lg`, `#0B192C`, tabular figures), trailing micro-trend or corridor indicator (e.g., "+4.2% Fleet Utilization" or "24h Avg Transit: Abuja-Kaduna"). Left edge includes an optional 3px vertical accent border in `#0B192C` or `#FF7A00` for critical fleet states.

### Data Tables & Waybill Logs
- Headers: Background `#F8FAFC`, bottom border 2px solid `#E2E8F0`, typography `label-md` in `#475569`, text uppercase.
- Rows: Background `#FFFFFF`, bottom border 1px solid `#F1F5F9`, row height 44px (dense) or 56px (standard). Hover state: `#F8FAFC`.
- Cells: Tabular-lined typography for weights (e.g., `32.4 MT`), container codes, and invoice totals.

### Chips & Compliance Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded: 9999px`), padding `0.125rem 0.625rem`, font `label-sm`.
- Status styles:
  - *Active Transit:* Light orange background (`#FFF7ED`), text `#C2410C`, border 1px solid `#FED7AA`.
  - *Compliant / Verified:* Light emerald background (`#ECFDF5`), text `#047857`, border 1px solid `#A7F3D0`.
  - *Restricted Corridor:* Light rose background (`#FEF2F2`), text `#B91C1C`, border 1px solid `#FECACA`.

### Interactive Quotation Request Widget
- Encapsulated in a `rounded-lg` panel featuring a distinct top accent strip (4px `#FF7A00`).
- Segmented cargo type buttons (Dry Bulk, Containerized, Heavy Machinery, FMCG) using unified radio-card patterns that toggle from border `#E2E8F0` to a deep navy border `#0B192C` with light slate background tint.
